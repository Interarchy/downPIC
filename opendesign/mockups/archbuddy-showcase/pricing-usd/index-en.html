FAQ
How are the 100 weekly uses counted?

They reset every Monday at 00:00 Beijing time. Successfully downloading and analyzing one new image counts once in the shared allowance. Re-downloading, browsing or organizing a saved image, and failed operations do not count. Downloading a new image alone also counts once; analyzing it later does not charge again. Free deep extraction has a separate 10-use weekly allowance that resets at the same time; failures do not count.

Do plan allowances and top-ups expire?

Plan analysis, extraction and BuddyCoin allowances expire at the end of the 30-day plan term; they are not replenished automatically. Separately purchased BuddyCoin does not expire or disappear when a plan ends. Prompt allowance packs are for holders of an active 30-day plan only; both allowances expire with the current 30-day plan term.

Can daily check-in credit pay for an image by itself?

Daily check-in BuddyCoin is valid that day and can cover at most 25% of a run, rounded down to 0.01 BuddyCoin. The remainder uses plan or purchased BuddyCoin. Check-in credit is spent first, then the plan balance closest to expiry, then purchased balance.

What happens if generation fails or times out?

If generation clearly fails without a result, reserved BuddyCoin is returned to its source. For timeouts or unknown status, the reservation remains while the job is checked, then is returned if failure is confirmed; it is never charged twice. Refunded expired check-in credit lasts until the end of the following day; refunded expired plan credit gets a 24-hour grace period. Partial delivery settles only the delivered portion.

What is BuddyCoin and how is it used?

BuddyCoin is ArchBuddy's creation credit. After choosing a model and resolution, the listed amount is deducted; for example, one Nano Banana 2.1 image at 4K costs 9 BuddyCoin. Plans add credits once at purchase, while top-ups add them once. Both use the same rate card and generation has no extra USD charge.

How do plans compare with top-ups?

The three plans cost $5.99 / 360, $9.99 / 720 and $19.99 / 1,620 BuddyCoin. Matching top-ups cost $7 / 360, $11 / 720 and $22 / 1,620 BuddyCoin, about 17%, 10% and 10% more. Plans also include analysis and extraction, while their BuddyCoin lasts only for the cycle; purchased BuddyCoin does not expire.

Is Library local or cloud based?

Free, Light and Standard Library data stays on the current device without cloud sync. Pro is proposed to add local images and cloud sync; cloud capacity, scope and timing remain to be determined. This page is a pricing proposal and cloud sync is not available yet.

Do plans renew automatically? Is there an annual plan?

This proposal allows a one-time 30-day purchase with no automatic renewal. You may purchase another term after expiry. There is no annual plan yet. Refund rules are in the Terms; payment methods and taxes are shown at checkout. These plans are not yet for sale.
