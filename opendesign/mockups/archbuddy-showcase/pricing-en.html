04 / DAILY CHECK-IN

A small daily boost

The proposed rule grants 10 bonus credits once per day. They expire at 23:59:59 Beijing time on the day claimed. At most 25% of the credits for any generation can come from check-in credits; the rest uses paid or subscription bonus credits. Check-in credits alone cannot pay for a generation.

The prices and rules on this page are a product proposal. Subscriptions, payments, and the shared account credit system are not live yet. Final terms will be confirmed before sales open.
