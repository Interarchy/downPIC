## 中文商品说明（可直接粘贴）

ArchBuddy 是连接参考图、提示词和画布创作的设计工具，适用于建筑、室内与景观设计工作。

在 Edge 网页中保存所选图片，或从图片浮框打开侧栏反推提示词；在完整图词库里按项目和分类整理图片、来源及已确认方案；使用参考图构建提示词，并结合生成结果继续优化。

官网、Canvas 与插件使用同一个 ArchBuddy 账户，可通过邮箱或 Google 登录。插件分别显示 BuddyCoin、简析和深度反推余额。普通图片下载免费，AI 功能需要登录及相应权益。

专业版提供 5 GB 云图词库，单张图片最多 20 MiB。只有主动选择的素材才保存到云端；可在同一账户的 Canvas 中使用。专业版当前为 USD 19.99 / 30 天一次性购买，不自动续费。云库素材在套餐有效期内持续保留，到期后有 30 天只读下载和续购期；普通画布图片仍按独立的 30 天规则保留。价格、权益和有效期以官网商品及账户页面为准。

支持 EN / 中一键切换弹窗、侧栏、图词库、云库和相关界面。扩展不自动全量上传本地素材，不进行广告追踪，不绕过网站登录或付费墙。所选图片、文本和账户数据的处理方式详见隐私说明。

## English description

ArchBuddy connects visual references, prompts and Canvas workflows for architecture, interior and landscape design.

Save selected web images, open the Edge sidebar for prompt extraction, organize references and confirmed prompts in the full library, and refine prompts with selected generated results. Use the same email or Google account as the ArchBuddy website and Canvas. BuddyCoin, brief-analysis and deep-reverse balances are shown separately; ordinary downloads are free.

Pro provides a 5 GB cloud library with a 20 MiB per-image limit. Only explicitly selected materials are saved to the cloud. Use them in Canvas with the same account. Pro is currently a USD 19.99 one-time purchase for 30 days, with no automatic renewal. Cloud materials are retained while the plan is active, then remain read-only for 30 days for download or renewal. Ordinary Canvas images have a separate 30-day retention rule.

AI features require an account and available allowances. Some features require a paid plan. Switch related extension interfaces with EN / 中. Local materials are not automatically uploaded in bulk. See the privacy notice for data processing and service providers.
